# Bizarrement Curieux - Channel Setup Kit

This document is the source of truth for creating and configuring the public
channels associated with the curious-question format. Copy public-facing text
from here instead of recreating it during each account setup.

Do not record private owner addresses, login aliases, phone numbers, passwords,
recovery codes, cookies, or tokens in this repository. Those belong in the
password manager. The addresses below describe roles, not credentials.

## Launch Order

1. Launch the French account, `Bizarrement Curieux`.

3. Reserve the Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese names when Google permits it.

## Account Architecture

- One stable Google Account may privately own multiple YouTube channels.
- Each public identity must be a distinct YouTube channel with its own name,
  handle, avatar, description, and language.
- A personal Google Account may own a public channel. Its personal name and
  email are not public channel fields unless they are deliberately exposed.
- Use the owner account only for ownership and recovery where possible.
- Use language-specific operational aliases for TikTok and Instagram logins.
- Use a dedicated public contact alias in profile contact fields. Never publish
  the owner address.
- Add a trusted backup owner when available. Do not share the owner password.
- Record the actual account-to-alias mapping in the password manager, not here.

## YouTube Setup

For every channel:

1. Create the channel with the exact public name above.
2. Claim the preferred handle or the first acceptable fallback.
3. Set the channel language and country deliberately.
4. Add the localized description.
5. Leave the public contact field empty until the dedicated contact alias is
   ready.
6. Upload the approved square avatar and banner. V1 placeholders may remain
   until the account art direction is validated.
7. Keep subscriptions private.
8. Enable two-step verification or a passkey on every owner account.
9. Configure YouTube Studio permissions instead of sharing credentials.
10. Inspect the public channel in a signed-out or private browser window.

Google may temporarily limit channel creation on new or inactive accounts. Do
not work around that restriction with disposable identities. Create the primary
French channel first, then add the other identities when the account is eligible.

## TikTok and Instagram Setup

For every language account:

- Use the same public name and preferred handle as YouTube when available.
- Use the localized short bio above.
- Keep the avatar, accent color, and account promise consistent within a
  language.
- Disable automatic cross-posting and profile synchronization between language
  accounts.
- Do not connect personal social profiles.
- Do not add personal names, locations, telephone numbers, or personal email
  addresses to public fields.
- Verify the final profile from another account before the first publication.

## Public Identity Rules

- The French and English accounts are sibling brands, not binary duplicates.
- Keep the production system and recognizable identity shared, but localize the
  script, hook, footage selection, callouts, outro, description, and metadata.
- Do not publish exported videos carrying another platform's watermark.
- Do not promise a fixed posting cadence until it is operationally sustainable.
- Do not call the content a test, prototype, AI experiment, or generated demo in
  public copy.
- Never claim that a video is fact-checked unless its claims and sources have
  actually passed the production QA.

## Privacy Check Before Launch

- [ ] Public name and handle contain no personal identifier.
- [ ] Avatar and banner contain no personal photo or hidden personal metadata.
- [ ] Description and links reveal no personal account.
- [ ] Public contact uses the intended brand alias.
- [ ] Subscriptions are private.
- [ ] The active avatar is checked before every comment or upload.
- [ ] Account owners and permissions are reviewed.
- [ ] Two-step verification or a passkey is enabled.
- [ ] Recovery codes are stored outside the repository.
- [ ] The signed-out channel page exposes only the intended brand information.
